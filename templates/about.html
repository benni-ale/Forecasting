What is DollarPunk?

A research prototype that tracks the digital sentiment around stocks in the financial news, and turns it into a single daily indicator per ticker.

How it works
  1. Collect. Financial news articles are ingested continuously (currently from the Alpha Vantage NEWS_SENTIMENT feed) and stored with their metadata: source, timestamp, tickers mentioned.
  2. Score. Every article carries, for each ticker it mentions, two numbers: a relevance score (0 to 1 — how central the ticker is in the article) and a sentiment score (−1 to +1 — how bearish or bullish the tone is toward that ticker).
  3. Decay. News gets old: the impact of an article fades over time. Each article's weight is halved every {{ default_kpi_half_life|int }} days (configurable half-life).
  4. Aggregate. For every ticker, all recent articles are combined into one daily sentiment KPI, weighting each article's sentiment by its relevance and its remaining freshness.
The KPI, precisely

For a given ticker and day, over the articles of the last {{ default_kpi_window }} days:

KPI  =  Σ ( sentiment × relevance × decay )  /  Σ ( relevance × decay )

decay = 0.5 ^ ( days_since_publication / {{ default_kpi_half_life|int }} )
KPI > 0
recent, relevant coverage is on balance bullish
KPI ≈ 0
mixed or neutral coverage
KPI < 0
recent, relevant coverage is on balance bearish
Reading the pages
Daily Dashboard
The main view. Ranks tickers by sentiment KPI (or by number of mentions) and shows, for each stock, the recent price/volume history and the sentiment KPI trend. Click any card to open the company details.
Companies
Browse the companies on file, with sector, industry and business description.
Articles
The raw material: every collected news article, searchable and filterable by ticker, with its per-ticker relevance and sentiment scores.
Company details
One page per ticker: price & volume chart, the sentiment KPI over time (same formula as the dashboard), company profile and the latest news mentioning the ticker.
Why

DollarPunk is the working prototype of a research project on NLP-driven market sentiment: whether the tone and intensity of financial news, aggregated with a time-decay model, carries a measurable signal about market behaviour. The roadmap includes independent sentiment scoring (FinBERT / LLM based), sentiment diffusion across semantically related companies, statistical sentiment–price analysis and a simple backtesting engine.

Not financial advice. This is an experimental research tool. Sentiment scores come from automated models, can be noisy or wrong, and past coverage patterns do not predict future prices. Do not base investment decisions on this dashboard.
Data: Alpha Vantage · Built with Flask + PostgreSQL · Charts: Chart.js
